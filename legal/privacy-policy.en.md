# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes what personal data the **Mestinghem AI** platform ("the Platform") collects, for which purposes, on what legal basis, and for how long it is stored. It is drawn up in line with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the laws of the Republic of Lithuania.

- Data controller: **Mestinghem AI UAB**
- Address: **Vilnius, Lithuania**
- Privacy contact: **privacy@mestinghem.ai**
- Data Protection Officer (DPO): **dpo@mestinghem.ai**
- Document version: 1.0 · current edition

## 1. What data we process

### 1.1. Data you provide
- Registration data: email address, username, display name, password (stored only as an irreversible cryptographic hash).
- Profile and preferences: interface language, theme, settings, and the module permissions you grant.
- Content you create: chat messages, uploaded and generated files, documents, images, chess games, terminal commands and their output.
- Device-control data (if you connect an agent): device name, platform, selected permission profile, and a log of requested and approved actions.

### 1.2. Data collected automatically
- Request telemetry: IP address, user-agent type, request time, response status.
- Security data: authentication events, failed sign-in attempts, security-policy triggers, session identifiers.
- Performance telemetry: module response times and feature-usage facts (without the content of your data).

### 1.3. Data we do NOT collect
We do not collect or process special categories of personal data (racial origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, health data, biometrics) and we do not covertly track users.

## 2. Purposes and legal bases

- Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): providing access to the Platform, running modules, storing your working materials, billing your plan.
- Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): marketing communications and optional features you choose to enable.
- Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): security, abuse and fraud prevention, keeping the service operational, improving quality.
- Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): maintaining the audit log and responding to lawful requests from competent authorities.

## 3. Artificial intelligence and the content of your requests

Requests you send to the chat and other AI modules are processed to generate a reply. Conversation content is used solely to provide the service to you. We do not use your private conversations, files or documents to train third-party models, and we do not share them for purposes unrelated to operating the Platform.

Replies are produced by compute capacity that may be provided by external inference providers. In that case only the text necessary to generate the reply is transmitted; providers are bound by confidentiality obligations and do not use the transmitted data for their own purposes. You may delete your conversation history and related files at any time.

## 4. Storage and geography

Personal data is stored in a managed cloud database and object storage located in jurisdictions with a level of protection recognised as adequate by the European Commission, or under Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC). We apply encryption in transit (TLS) and least-privilege access control.

## 5. Retention periods

- Account and profile: until you delete your account.
- Conversation history, files, documents, games: until you delete them or delete your account.
- Session tokens: no longer than their lifetime (short-lived); revoked tokens are deleted.
- Audit log and security events: up to 12 months, unless a longer period is required by law.
- After account deletion: data is anonymised or deleted within 30 days, except for records the law requires us to keep.

## 6. Disclosure to third parties

We do not sell personal data. Disclosure may occur only to:
- vetted infrastructure providers (hosting, database, storage, inference) to the extent necessary to run the service;
- professional advisers (lawyers, auditors) under confidentiality;
- competent public authorities where there is a legal basis.

All processors are engaged under agreements complying with Art. 28 GDPR.

## 7. Cookies and similar technologies

The Platform uses a limited set of cookies and browser local storage necessary for authentication and for saving language and theme preferences. See the Cookie Policy for details.

## 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right:
- of access to your data and to obtain a copy;
- to rectification of inaccurate data;
- to erasure ("right to be forgotten");
- to restriction of processing;
- to data portability (export in a machine-readable format);
- to object to processing based on legitimate interest;
- to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing;
- to lodge a complaint with the State Data Protection Inspectorate of Lithuania (Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija).

To exercise your rights, use the security section of your profile (export and deletion are available in the interface) or write to **privacy@mestinghem.ai**. We respond within 30 days.

## 9. Children's data

The Platform is not intended for persons under 16 without parental consent. If we become aware of a violation, the account will be deleted.

## 10. Changes to this policy

For material changes we will notify users by email or in the interface no later than 14 days before they take effect.
