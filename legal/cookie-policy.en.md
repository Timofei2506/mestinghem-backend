# Cookie Policy

This Policy explains which cookies and similar technologies the **Mestinghem AI** platform uses, why, and how you can manage them.

- Operator: **Mestinghem AI UAB**, Vilnius, Lithuania
- Contact: **privacy@mestinghem.ai**
- Version: 1.0

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a site stores in your browser. Alongside cookies we use browser local storage, which works similarly but keeps data only on your device.

## 2. Categories we use

### 2.1. Strictly necessary
The Platform cannot function without them. They do not require consent because they are necessary to provide the service you requested.
- Authentication token and session state — to keep you signed in.
- Secure HttpOnly/SameSite attributes to prevent session hijacking.

### 2.2. Functional
Remember your preferences to make the interface more convenient.
- Interface language (Russian or English).
- Theme and animation settings.
- Active workspace identifier (for example, the current chess game).

### 2.3. Analytics (limited)
Help us understand which features are used so we can improve the service. We use our own aggregated telemetry without personal profiling and without sharing data with advertising networks.

## 3. What we do NOT do

- We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
- We do not share cookie data with ad platforms.
- We do not build advertising profiles of users.

## 4. Retention

Session cookies are deleted when the browser closes. Functional preferences in local storage persist until you reset them. Security tokens have a limited lifetime and are rotated regularly.

## 5. Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent sign-in. Resetting local storage returns language and visual settings to defaults.

## 6. Changes

If the set of technologies changes we will update this Policy and, where required, re-request consent for optional categories.
