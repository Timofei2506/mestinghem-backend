# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is entered into between **Mestinghem AI UAB** ("Processor") and the customer ("Controller") under Art. 28 GDPR and governs the processing of personal data on the Controller's behalf when using the **Mestinghem AI** platform.

- Version: 1.0
- Contact: **dpo@mestinghem.ai**

## 1. Subject matter and scope

The Processor processes personal data solely on behalf of and under the documented instructions of the Controller, to the extent necessary to provide the service. The subject of processing is providing access to the Platform, storing working materials, and ensuring security and support.

## 2. Nature and purpose of processing

- Purpose: performance of the service agreement.
- Nature: storage, organisation, transfer within the infrastructure, deletion.
- Categories of data: registration and profile data, content of the user's working materials, request telemetry, security events.
- Data subjects: the Controller's representatives and end users to whom the Controller granted access.

## 3. Processor obligations

The Processor undertakes to:
- process data only on the Controller's documented instructions, unless required by law;
- ensure confidentiality and access only by authorised personnel bound by obligations;
- apply technical and organisational measures under Art. 32 GDPR (encryption in transit, access control, logging, backups);
- engage subprocessors only in accordance with section 6;
- assist the Controller in responding to data-subject requests and in impact assessments and breach notifications;
- delete or return data at the end of services at the Controller's choice;
- provide information necessary to demonstrate compliance and allow reasonable audits.

## 4. Controller obligations

The Controller is responsible for the lawfulness of collection, the existence of legal bases, informing data subjects and the accuracy of data provided, and for issuing instructions that comply with the law.

## 5. Security measures

The Processor applies the measures described in the "Security and Data Protection" document, including least-privilege access control, connection encryption, user data isolation, security-event logging, incident-response procedures and regular backups.

## 6. Subprocessors

The Controller grants general prior authorisation to engage subprocessors providing infrastructure services: hosting, managed database, object storage, and compute for generating responses. The Processor notifies changes to the list no later than 14 days in advance and enters into an agreement with each subprocessor ensuring no lesser level of protection. The current list is available on request at **dpo@mestinghem.ai**.

## 7. Transfers to third countries

Where processing involves transfers outside the EEA, they are made under an adequacy decision or Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) with supplementary protective measures.

## 8. Breach notification

The Processor notifies the Controller of a personal-data breach without undue delay, where possible within 48 hours of becoming aware, and provides the information the Controller needs to meet its obligations to the supervisory authority and data subjects.

## 9. Assistance with data-subject rights

The Processor provides technical assistance to the Controller in responding to data-subject requests (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection) using Platform features, including data export and deletion.

## 10. Term

This DPA remains in force for the entire period of processing and survives until the Controller's data is fully deleted or returned.

## 11. Liability

Each party is liable within the limits set by the GDPR and applicable law.

## 12. Governing law

This DPA is governed by the laws of Lithuania.
