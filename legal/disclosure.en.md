# Vulnerability Disclosure

We take the security of the **Mestinghem AI** platform seriously and value the help of the research community. If you discover a vulnerability, please tell us — we will review it promptly.

- Contact: **security@mestinghem.ai**
- Version: 1.0

## 1. How to report

Send an email with the subject "Vulnerability report". Include:
- a description of the issue and its potential impact;
- step-by-step reproduction or a proof of concept (PoC);
- affected components, versions or URLs (without disclosing other users' data);
- your contact details for follow-up.

We acknowledge receipt within 3 business days and aim to give a substantive response within 14 days.

## 2. Safe harbour

We ask you to:
- research only on your own accounts and devices;
- not access or modify other users' data;
- not disrupt service availability (denial of service, stress tests without coordination);
- not publish details before agreeing on a disclosure date;
- give us reasonable time to fix the issue before public disclosure.

If you follow these rules, we will not pursue legal action for good-faith research.

## 3. What not to do

- Do not use automated scanners that create high load.
- Do not apply social engineering against staff or users.
- Do not test physical systems or offices.

## 4. Scope

In scope are the Platform's web application, its public APIs and the official device-control agent. Third-party services not operated by us should be reported to their owners.

## 5. Timelines and publication

We aim to fix critical vulnerabilities as a priority. After a patch is released we may agree on joint publication and credit the researcher (with their consent) in acknowledgements.

## 6. Encrypted communication

For confidential correspondence you may request our PGP key by writing to **security@mestinghem.ai**.

Thank you for helping make Mestinghem AI safer.
