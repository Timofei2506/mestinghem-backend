# Acceptable Use Policy

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) defines how the **Mestinghem AI** platform may and may not be used. It complements the Terms of Service. Breaches may lead to feature restrictions, account suspension or data deletion.

- Operator: **Mestinghem AI UAB**, Vilnius, Lithuania
- Version: 1.0

## 1. Prohibited use

You may not use the Platform to:
- conduct any activity that violates the law of Lithuania, the EU or international norms;
- create, distribute or store unlawful content;
- commit fraud, deceive or mislead, or pass automated content off as human where that causes harm;
- generate malicious software, exploits or attack tooling;
- send mass mailings or spam, or coordinate audience manipulation;
- infringe third-party intellectual property or confidentiality;
- collect other people's personal data without a legal basis;
- attack information systems, or scan and intrude without the owner's permission;
- circumvent the Platform's security controls, limits or policies;
- exploit the service in ways that could harm people, including minors.

## 2. Terminal and agent use

- The terminal is intended for tasks within your workspace, not for attacking external systems.
- The device-control agent may be installed only on equipment you own or lawfully manage.
- You may not use the agent for covert surveillance, input interception, credential harvesting or other actions without the device owner's knowledge.

## 3. Generated content

- You are responsible for how you use generated content.
- Do not use the Platform to create disinformation, forgeries or content that infringes third-party rights.
- Comply with labelling requirements for synthetic content where established.

## 4. Load and automation

Do not abuse compute resources: creating many automated accounts, circumventing request limits, or using the service for cryptocurrency mining and other resource-intensive tasks unrelated to the Platform's purpose.

## 5. Moderation and sanctions

We apply automated and manual checks to detect violations. Depending on severity, measures may include: a warning, request rejection, temporary feature lock, account suspension or deletion, and, where necessary, referral to competent authorities.

## 6. Reporting abuse

If you discover abuse of the Platform, report it to **support@mestinghem.ai**.
